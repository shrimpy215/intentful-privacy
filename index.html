Privacy Policy

Intentful Planner · Last updated: September 2026

The short version. Intentful Planner is built to keep your data yours. Everything you enter lives on your device and, if you turn it on, in your own iCloud account — not on our servers. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use third-party analytics or tracking. Your information only leaves your device when you use the optional AI features or two-way calendar sync, as described below.

1. What this covers

This policy describes how the Intentful Planner app (“the App”) handles your information on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

2. Data stored on your device and in your iCloud

Everything you enter — goals, habits, journal entries, priorities, wins, projects, vision-board images, and settings — is stored locally on your device. If you are signed into iCloud, the App uses Apple's CloudKit to sync this content across your own devices through your private iCloud account. That synced data lives in Apple's iCloud infrastructure under your account; we (the developer) cannot see or access it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Calendar and Reminders

With your permission, the App reads from and writes to your Apple Calendar and Apple Reminders so your events and tasks stay in one place. This information stays on your device and within your Apple account — it is not sent to us. You can revoke this access at any time in your device's Settings, and you can disconnect sync from within the App.

4. AI features (AI Coach and Vision Board)

These features are optional. When you use them:

  • AI Coach. The messages you send, and the planner context needed to answer them, are transmitted through our secure proxy service to Anthropic (the Claude AI provider), which generates the response.
  • Vision Board. The text prompt you provide is transmitted through our proxy to OpenAI to generate an image.

Our proxy forwards these requests to the AI provider and returns the result. It does not store the content of your prompts or the AI's responses — it records only operational metadata (such as timestamps, latency, and a non-identifying hashed token used for rate limiting). The AI providers process your request under their own API terms; per those terms, API inputs are not used to train their models. Generated images are stored on your device and in your iCloud like your other content.

Bring your own key. If you enter your own API key in Settings, those requests go directly from your device to the provider and do not pass through our proxy. Your API keys are stored securely in your device's Keychain and are never transmitted to us.

5. Subscription, trial, and purchases

Intentful Planner is offered as an auto-renewing subscription, managed entirely by Apple through the App Store. New users begin with a free trial; when the trial ends, continued use of the App requires an active subscription. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, and the subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your Apple Account settings, and the exact trial length and price are shown in the App before you subscribe.

We do not receive or store your payment information. To confirm an active subscription for the AI features, the App sends Apple's signed transaction record to our proxy for authentication; this contains no personal payment details, and we log only a hashed, non-identifying token derived from it, retained for rate limiting and then automatically deleted.

Use of the App is also subject to Apple's standard End User License Agreement.

6. What we can and cannot see

We do not have access to your planner content, your journal, your calendar or reminders, or your iCloud data. Our servers exist only to relay the optional AI requests and to verify subscriptions, and they are configured to log metadata only — never the content of your entries, messages, or generated images.

7. Advertising, analytics, and diagnostics

The App contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data. If you choose to share diagnostics with developers (an Apple setting), Apple may provide us with crash and performance reports; these help us fix problems and contain no personal content from your planner.

8. Third-party services we rely on

The App relies on Apple (iCloud sync, the App Store, and subscriptions), Anthropic (AI Coach), OpenAI (Vision Board image generation), and Amazon Web Services (hosting our proxy). Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

9. Your controls and data retention

Because your content lives on your device and in your iCloud, you stay in control of it. You can delete individual items; use Delete All Data in Settings to erase everything (which also removes it from your iCloud); disconnect Calendar and Reminders sync; revoke permissions in your device Settings; and manage or cancel your subscription through Apple. We do not retain your content on our servers. Cancelling your subscription does not delete anything — your data stays on your device and in your iCloud until you remove it.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new “last updated” date.
